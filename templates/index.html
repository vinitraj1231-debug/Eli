Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 1, 2024

1. Introduction

At EliteHosting.in ("we," "our," "us"), accessible from https://elitehosting.in, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information collected through our platform. This Privacy Policy outlines what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is secured, and your rights as a user.

2. Personal Information Collected

We collect only necessary information required to provide, secure, and operate our hosting services:

  • Account Data: Username, email address, hashed account password, and assigned referral code.
  • Technical & Log Data: Registration IP address, last logged-in IP address, browser user-agent, and rate-limiting request timestamps.
  • Payment & Billing Data: Transaction ID / UTR reference number, payment amount, purchased VPS slot tier, and wallet balance history. (We never collect or store raw credit/debit card numbers, CVV, OTPs, or UPI PINs).
  • Application Code Data: GitHub repository links, uploaded ZIP archives, single script files, and environment variable key-value pairs configured for deployments.

3. How & Why We Collect Data

Your data is collected directly when you register an account, log in, submit a payment UTR, deploy an application, or configure environment variables. We use this data to:

  • Provision and execute your containerized VPS deployments and web routes.
  • Process and verify manual or automated payment requests and wallet top-ups.
  • Prevent platform abuse, brute-force attacks, DDoS attempts, and unauthorized access.
  • Comply with applicable Indian IT laws and CERT-In cyber security directives.

4. Secrets Vault & Container Isolation

Sensitive environment variables (such as TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN or database URIs) are encrypted in transit and in storage. Secrets are injected directly into the runtime memory of your container process and are never stored in plain text files on host disks. Hard namespace virtualization prevents cross-tenant access to SQLite session files or execution directories.

5. Cookies & Session Management

We use essential, HTTP-only session cookies (session) solely to authenticate logged-in users and secure forms against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF). We do not use third-party advertising or invasive cross-site tracking cookies.

6. Third-Party Integrations

We integrate with GitHub for public/private repository imports and utilize Telegram for official customer support (@zolvid). Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose information only if required by law enforcement or Indian court orders in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000.

8. User Rights & Data Deletion Process

You have the right to access, modify, or permanently delete your account and deployment data. To request full deletion of your account, deployments, or payment records, contact us at support@elitehosting.in or via Telegram @zolvid. Account deletion requests are processed within 48 hours.

9. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries, contact our Privacy Officer at support@elitehosting.in or Telegram @zolvid.
