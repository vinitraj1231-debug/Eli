EliteHosting is India's premium, developer-first cloud deployment platform. We provide high-performance, cost-effective hosting with support for instant deployment from GitHub or ZIP files, dynamic port allocation, and automatic language detection.

We are fully optimized for the Indian developer ecosystem and support secure UPI payments including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM QR codes. Your credit purchases are verified and processed instantly upon admin approval.

EliteHosting uses a simple, transparent credit system: 1 credit = 1 active deployment. We offer fixed pricing tiers starting at ₹99 for 2 credits, ₹199 for 5 credits, and ₹299 for 8 credits. Credits are permanent and only consumed when initiating a deployment.

We offer a generous referral program: when a developer registers using your referral code and purchases premium credits, you automatically receive a 30% commission directly into your wallet balance. You can request direct withdrawal of your earnings anytime!

Security is our top priority. Our system uses a secure, database-backed Secret Injection framework that mounts your configurations directly into the memory processes of your scripts at execution time. Secrets are never saved to raw disk files, protecting you from GitHub leakages.

We support out-of-the-box hosting for Python 3 and Node.js. Our system automatically detects files like main.py, app.py, server.py, index.js, and server.js, and executes package managers (like pip and npm) for dynamic dependencies.
