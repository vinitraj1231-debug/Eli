Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: March 2026

This policy clearly outlines the terms governing cancellations, refunds, and failed transactions for digital courses purchased on Elite Academy India.

1. Refund Eligibility for Digital Courses

Because our courses are digital products with immediate download/access privileges upon payment, refunds are handled strictly under the following fair conditions:

  • 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee: You are eligible for a 100% full refund within 7 days of purchase if you have consumed less than 25% of the total course video lessons and have not downloaded source code assets.
  • Technical Non-Delivery: If you paid successfully via PayU but technical issues prevented you from accessing the course content, and our support team is unable to resolve the issue within 48 hours, a full refund will be issued.

2. Non-Refundable Situations

A refund will NOT be issued in the following cases:

  • If the refund request is submitted after 7 days from the purchase date.
  • If you have watched/completed more than 25% of the course modules.
  • If you have downloaded the complete course source code or project repository.
  • If your account is suspended due to copyright infringement or reselling violations.

3. Refund Request Procedure & Required Details

To request a refund, please send an email to support@elitehosting.in with the subject line "Refund Request - [Order ID]".

You must provide the following information:

  1. Order ID (e.g. ORD-A1B2C3D4)
  2. PayU Transaction ID / Payment Reference Number
  3. Registered Email Address & Phone Number
  4. Reason for the refund request

4. Processing Timeline & Refund Method

Once your refund request is verified and approved, it will be processed within 2 to 3 business days. The refunded amount will be credited back to your original payment method (Bank Account, Credit/Debit Card, UPI, or Wallet) through PayU within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank's processing cycle.

5. Failed or Pending Transaction Refunds

In rare cases where your money was debited from your bank/UPI account but the payment failed or remained pending on PayU, the funds are automatically reversed by your bank back to your account within 3 to 5 business days. If you need assistance verifying transaction status, contact support at support@elitehosting.in.
